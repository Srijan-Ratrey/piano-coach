    Your file is read in the browser and never uploaded. Arrangements of modern songs are copyrighted — fine to practise with, not to redistribute.

    2 Which hand?

    One hand first — one stream, one verification target.

    3 How much of it?

    Every note of a step must sound before it advances, so a big-chord arrangement can be unclearable one note at a time. Thinning is the way through — and dense chords are where detection is weakest anyway.

    4 Start

    Choose a song above first.

    What this cannot do. Fingering numbers are guidance and are never checked — a microphone cannot see which finger you used. “Any song” means any MIDI file, not any recording. And how well detection works depends on your piano, your room and your microphone.

    The on‑screen keyboard highlights what to play. It is never an input — only the microphone is.
